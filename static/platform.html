    What does an address actually cost?

    Count requests, not keystrokes.

    One autocomplete request costs 1 credit, whether it returns one suggestion, several suggestions, or no matches. Typing one address can send several requests.

    A short address search

    3 autocomplete requests while entering the street + 1 after adding the house number = 4 credits.

    Selecting a displayed suggestion costs nothing by itself. If your integration then validates the address, add 3 credits: 7 credits total.

    An order from a business

    4 autocomplete requests + 1 validation + 1 company lookup:

    4 × 1 + 1 × 3 + 1 × 5 = 12 credits

    At that exact usage, 100 orders consume 1,200 credits. Starter covers that volume for €9/month.

    Debouncing means a fast burst of typing can become one request; a pause or edit can send another. Every new successful request counts, including repeat or cached lookups. A completed validation costs 3 credits even if the address needs review; a completed company lookup costs 5 even if no company is found. Authentication, invalid-input, server and quota errors consume no credits. A request aborted in the browser can still count if the server has already completed it. Both minimal and full company responses cost 5 credits, including the VAT check.

    The public demos above use a separate demo key. Their requests do not use your customer credits.
